About Jack Johnson

I run MediaJack Systems, a one-person marketing firm in Englewood, Colorado. I plan, make and run marketing, in print and online, for small and growing businesses.

A career on the selling side

I started out selling advertising to local businesses across the country. That job teaches you fast what owners care about: whether the phone rings, what it costs and whether the person who sold it to you picks up afterward.

From there I spent seven years at a Texas energy company running lead generation, investor relations and every presentation that went with them, and built two company platforms from the ground up.

Today I'm also the part-time marketing lead, or fractional CMO, for three companies. MediaJack Systems brings the same work to businesses that need marketing done right without hiring a department.
