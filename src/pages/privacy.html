Privacy

Privacy

Short version: this site has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. If you call, email or send me a message, I use what you send only to reply to you and to do the work you ask for.

What this website collects

Nothing unless you send it. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party scripts on this site. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as the address a request came from and the page asked for, to keep the site running and secure.

The message form

If you use the form on the contact or check-up page, what you type is sent to my inbox by Resend, the service that delivers the email. The website itself keeps no copy. The eight questions on the check-up page run in your browser, and your answers go nowhere unless you choose to add them to your message.

Booking a call

The booking link opens a Google Calendar page run by Google. What you enter there, such as your name, email address and phone number, goes to my calendar so I can call you, and Google's own privacy policy applies to that page.

When you contact me

If you email, call or send a message, I keep it, your contact details and anything you send me, so I can answer you and do the work you ask for. I don't sell or rent your information, and I don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask to be on one.

Emails from MediaJack Systems

If you receive a sales email from me and don't want another, reply "no thanks" or "unsubscribe" and you won't hear from me again.

Questions or requests

To ask what I hold about you, or to have it deleted, email jack@mediajacksystems.com or write to MediaJack Systems LLC, 6483 S Havana St, Unit B, Englewood, CO 80111.
