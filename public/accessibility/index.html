Accessibility

Accessibility

This site is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, and to be easy to use on any phone or computer.

What that means here

  • Text contrast well above the AA minimum, with body text above 7 to 1.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a link to skip to the main content.
  • The words on the signs are real text, so screen readers read them and they grow when you zoom.
  • Headings, landmarks and labels that screen readers can follow.
  • Text you can enlarge without losing content, and layouts that work from small phones to wide screens.
  • The form has a visible label on every field and explains any mistake in words.
  • The light that passes over the signs is the only thing that moves, and it is turned off if your device asks for reduced motion.

How it is checked

Every page is scanned with axe-core at phone and desktop widths and tested by keyboard before a change is published.
