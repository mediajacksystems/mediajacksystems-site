Privacy

Short version: this site has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. If you call or email me, I use what you send only to reply to you and to do the work you ask for.

What this website collects

Nothing on purpose. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels, no forms and no third-party scripts on this site. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as the address a request came from and the page asked for, to keep the site running and secure.

When you contact me

If you email or call, I keep your message, your contact details and anything you send me, so I can answer you and do the work you ask for. I don't sell or rent your information, and I don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask to be on one.

Emails from MediaJack Systems

If you receive a sales email from me and don't want another, reply "no thanks" or "unsubscribe" and you won't hear from me again.

Questions or requests

To ask what I hold about you, or to have it deleted, email jack@mediajacksystems.com or write to MediaJack Systems LLC, 6483 S Havana St, Unit B, Englewood, CO 80111.
