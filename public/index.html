Questions owners ask

What does it cost?

Websites start at $500, and hosting with small updates is $50 a month. Setting up a social platform with 4 starting posts is $150, and 12 posts a month is $100 a month. For bigger businesses, websites start at $1,500. The check-up is free. See all prices.

I already have a website. Can you just fix it?

Yes. I fix what's wrong with the site you have for a flat $295. It's done within a week, and you pay when it's done. The free check-up tells you what needs fixing first.

Do you only work with Colorado businesses?

No. I work with businesses across the US by phone, email and video calls. Around Denver we can also meet in person.

Can you take on just one thing?

Yes. Plenty of work starts with one mailer, one sign or one website fix. If it goes well, you'll know who to call for the next thing.

What is a custom system?

Software built for your business when ready-made tools don't fit. For a freight broker I built a rate checker, a fuel surcharge check and a map of nearly 150,000 places for their drivers. Tell me what you need and I'll quote it in writing.

What is a fractional CMO?

A part-time head of marketing. You get someone who sets the plan and budget, keeps the calendar moving and manages the vendors, for a monthly fee instead of a full-time salary.

Do you handle the printing too?

Yes. I design it and get it printed, and for direct mail I get it mailed. You don't have to deal with the printer or the post office.
