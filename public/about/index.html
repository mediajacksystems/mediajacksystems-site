About

About Jack Johnson

I run MediaJack Systems, a one-person marketing firm in Englewood, Colorado. I do marketing for local businesses, in print and online. When you call, you get me.

A career on the selling side

I started out selling advertising to local businesses across the country. That job teaches you fast what owners care about: whether the phone rings, what it costs and whether the person who sold it to you picks up afterward.

From there I spent seven years at a Texas energy company running lead generation, investor relations and every presentation that went with them, and built two company platforms from the ground up.

Today I'm also the part-time marketing lead, or fractional CMO, for three companies. MediaJack Systems does the same work for businesses too small to hire a marketing department.

What you can hold me to

A written price before I start.
Each quote lists what's included and when it'll be done. If the job changes, you get a new quote first.
No made-up numbers.
If I tell you something worked, I'll show you where the number came from. If something didn't work, you'll hear that too.
Print and digital together.
I plan the mailer, the sign, the website and the Google listing together, so they say the same thing.
One person to call.
I do the work or manage the people who do it, and I'm the one who answers when you have a question.
